Class VIII Scholar Badge

Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram · Academic year 2025–26

About the achievement

I was awarded the Scholar Badge for Class VIII for the academic year 2025–26, in recognition of my academic and co-curricular achievements. This was my fourth Scholar Badge in a row, after earning it in Class V, Class VI and Class VII.

The ceremony

Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram organised the Scholar Badge Ceremony for Class IX on 29 September 2026, celebrating the dedication, perseverance and academic achievements of students who demonstrated a consistent commitment to learning and excellence.

The school had the privilege of welcoming the esteemed Chief Guest, Dr. Harsh Mahajan, Member of the DPS Society. An eminent radiologist and the Founder and Managing Director of Mahajan Imaging, Dr. Mahajan is a distinguished medical professional who has been conferred several prestigious awards and accolades, including the Padma Shri in 2002. He has also served as a consultant and advisor to several important national and international organisations, including the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC).
