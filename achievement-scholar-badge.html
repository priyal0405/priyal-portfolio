Class VIII Scholar Badge

Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram · Academic year 2024–25

About the achievement

I was awarded the Scholar Badge for Class VIII for the academic year 2024–25, in recognition of my academic and co-curricular achievements. This was my third Scholar Badge in a row, after earning it in Class VI and Class VII.

The ceremony

The Class VIII Scholar Badge Ceremony was held at Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram, on 26 August 2025, in the school’s Audio-Visual Hall.

The ceremony began with the lighting of the lamp by the Chief Guest, Dr. Pallavi Jain Govil, Secretary, Youth Affairs, Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India, along with the Principal, Mr. Anil Kumar; the Vice Principals, Mr. Naresh Miglani, Mr. Anil Kathuria and Mr. Mukesh Kumar; and the Headmistresses, Ms. Rashmi Malhotra and Dr. Anubha Gopal.
