About the achievement

On 9 October 2026, Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram held the graduation ceremony of the first batch of students from ΩZω – the AI Innovation and Incubation Centre. The ceremony

The ceremony was attended by the Principal, Mr. Anil Kumar; the Vice Principals, Mr. Naresh Miglani, Mr. Anil Kathuria, Mr. Mukesh Kumar and Ms. Rashmi Malhotra; and the Headmistresses, Dr. Anubha Gopal and Ms. Vandana Chandhoke; along with Mr. Ajay Goel, Head, Academics, IBDP, and Mr. Manoj Biswal, Head of AI Practice at VVDN Technologies.
