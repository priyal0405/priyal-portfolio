Class VI Scholar Badge

Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram

About the achievement

I was awarded the Scholar Badge for Class VI, in recognition of my academic and co-curricular achievements.

I was travelling at the time of the ceremony and couldn’t attend, so my class teacher presented the badge to me personally afterwards.

This was the first of three Scholar Badges in a row. I went on to earn it again in Class VII and Class VIII.
