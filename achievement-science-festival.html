Volunteer & Presenter, Festival della Scienza 5.0

Inter-School Science Festival, DPS R. K. Puram · 18 July 2026

About the achievement

I volunteered at Festival della Scienza 5.0 – Speciale IA, and presented a game we made to show the harmful effects of viruses on the human body: Virus vs Immunity.

The game was played by two teams. The Virus team tried to infect the body, while the Immunity team tried to protect and defend it.

The festival

DPS R. K. Puram celebrated innovation through Festival della Scienza 5.0 – Speciale IA (Artificial Intelligence), an Inter-School Science Festival held on 18 July 2026. Bringing together students of Classes VI to XII from 22 schools across Delhi NCR, the festival provided a dynamic platform to showcase scientific knowledge, creativity and innovation.

Centred on the theme “Artificial Intelligence: Shaping the Future through Science and Innovation,” the event encouraged participants to explore AI’s role across scientific disciplines while fostering critical thinking, collaboration, ethical awareness and responsible innovation.

A wide range of engaging competitions was organised for students across different classes. The event was enriched by the presence of luminaries from the field of academics, including Dr. Nimish Joseph, Professor of Practice (Information Systems and AI), St. Stephen’s College, New Delhi; Dr. Dileswar Nayak, Associate Professor, School of Environmental Sciences, JNU; Mr. Kameswara Sharma, Associate Professor, Sri Venkateswara College; Dr. Rakhi Narang, Associate Professor, Sri Venkateswara College; and Dr. Anil Kumar, Associate Professor, Sri Venkateswara College.
