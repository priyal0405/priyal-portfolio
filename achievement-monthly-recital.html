Solo Performance at Theme’s Monthly Recital

Theme, GK 2 · Solo piano performance · “A Thousand Years”

About the performance

It was a solo performance, and I absolutely crushed it! It’s been so long since my last solo performance (two years), so this was necessary. Sure, I’ve done bigger concerts, but they’ve all been group performances, and it doesn’t hit the same.

I played “A Thousand Years”, a bit of a switch from my usual classical pieces.

Oddly enough, I wasn’t nervous at all. Usually I’m nervous at the start and get better as I play, but this time it was different.

This was also my first time playing on a grand piano. I’ve played on all kinds of electric pianos, keyboards and acoustic pianos, but never a grand piano (till now).

I got so many compliments! Even while I was going back to my seat, so many people asked me what grade I’m in and said it was amazing. Even the host said, “Wow, a great performance! I’m sure we all enjoyed it!”

I felt awesome about my performance.
