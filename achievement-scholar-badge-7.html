Class VII Scholar Badge

Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram

About the achievement

I was awarded the Scholar Badge for Class VII, in recognition of my academic and co-curricular achievements. This was my second Scholar Badge in a row, after Class VI, and I went on to earn it again the following year, in Class VIII.

The ceremony

The Class VII Scholar Badge Ceremony was held at Delhi Public School, R. K. Puram, in the presence of the Chief Guest, Mr. Anshul Gupta, Director (RMD), The Times of India; the Presiding Patron of the Ceremony, Mr. Sanjiv Kaura, President, Times Foundation; and Ms. Deepti Mehra, Vice President, The Times of India.

They were joined by the Principal, Mr. Anil Kumar; the Vice Principals, Mr. Naresh Miglani, Mr. Anil Kathuria and Mr. Mukesh Kumar; and the Headmistresses, Ms. Rashmi Malhotra and Dr. Anubha Gopal.
