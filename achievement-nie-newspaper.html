MudraPragyan AI Featured in the NIE Newspaper

“Innovation powered by AI” · NIE School Scoop, 24 September 2026 · Also shared on the DPS R. K. Puram website

The article

On 24 September 2026, the NIE newspaper’s School Scoop page covered the ceremony where DPS R. K. Puram’s Omega Zen Omega (ΩZω) AI Innovation and Incubation Centre celebrated the AI projects built by its students and recognised the top three achievers of its graduating batch.

The article mentions MudraPragyan AI, the project whose website I built, as one of the shortlisted teams whose AI prototypes were exhibited at the ceremony, alongside ASCEND, ReyCool, Examzy and BiblioTech.

The article was also shared on the DPS R. K. Puram school website.
