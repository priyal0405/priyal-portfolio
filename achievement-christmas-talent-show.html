About the achievement

I won first place out of 50+ participants at the Gulmohar Community Christmas talent show in 2018, playing a fusion of Christmas carols on the keyboard. I received a Certificate of Achievement at the event.

It was one of my earliest performances, and the start of my journey in music that continues today with my piano studies.
