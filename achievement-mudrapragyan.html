MudraPragyan AI · AI Innovation & Incubation Centre, DPS R. K. Puram · Batch 1

The project

MudraPragyan.AI is an AI-powered website that translates ASL (American Sign Language) hand gestures into text in real time, using computer vision and machine learning.

The platform helps bridge communication gaps between sign language users and people unfamiliar with sign language, through an accessible and user-friendly interface.
